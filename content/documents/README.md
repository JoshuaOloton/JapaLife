# documents/

`documents.json` lists the documents a player applies for: name, which rule in `rules/nigeria.json` holds its real fee, wait and validity, what the player must hold first (`requires`, listed before the document that needs it), the ledger reason for its fee, and whether a scam "agent" tempts the player (`agentOffer`). Also the expiry warning and the agent's terms.

Offices are `document_office` places in the city plan, each naming the documents it handles.
